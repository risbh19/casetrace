# Demo walkthrough

Use the synthetic fixtures and the local officer accounts. The walkthrough is designed for about three minutes.

| Time | Screen | What to show |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Case register | Sign in as `demo.io`, search for `DEMO-FIR-001` and open the case. |
| 0:21 | Evidence | Review the FIR, extracted entities and original-file hash. |
| 0:44 | Investigation plan | Generate the checklist, open an official citation and mark the first step done. |
| 1:04 | Money trail | Follow the synthetic transfers and identify the terminal balance marked for review. |
| 1:28 | Deadlines and legal library | Calculate a progress reminder and look up its section. |
| 1:51 | Draft notice | Save a bank-statement draft as the investigating officer. |
| 2:17 | Supervisor review | Sign in as `demo.sho`, approve the draft and record the simulated send. |
| 2:45 | Activity | Show the separate audit entries for drafting, approval and simulated delivery. |
